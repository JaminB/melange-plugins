## 1.1.0

- Knockback for the Buddy Bomb, the labradoodle that takes the Sheep's slot in Kindjal 1.2.0: 0.85 over 200. His
  blast is bigger than the Plague Ram's, so this is more than the Plague Ram's 0.70 over 170, the number the Sheep had
  before.
- The dependency on Kindjal is now written `kindjal >=1.2.0`. 1.0.0 wrote it with a space (`kindjal >= 1.1.0`),
  which Melange could not read, so the add-on did not load. It now needs Kindjal 1.2.0 or later.

## 1.0.0

First release. Needs Melange 0.9.0 or later and Kindjal 1.1.0 or later.
