## 1.3.0

Weapons now unlock as the match progresses and run out after a few uses, in both styles.

- Kanly: weapons have an unlock turn (Delay) in four tiers, start, turn 1, turn 2 and turn 3. The match opens with basics
  and utilities; mid-tier weapons come at turn 1, heavy ones at turn 2, and the super weapons (Concrete Donkey, Super
  Airstrike, Fatkins, Starburst, Flood, Alien Abduction, Weapon Factory weapon) at turn 3.
- Ammo is cut hard: basics 3 to 4, utilities 2 to 3, mid tier 2 (power-ups 1), heavy and super weapons 1. Nothing is
  unlimited and crates stay on.
- The 15 mystery crate entries now get 1 ammo and unlock at turn 1.
- Kanly Melee: the same tiers for the allowed weapons (no super tier), same bans.
- Netplay: the host and every peer need Kanly 1.3.0.

## 1.2.0

Adds a second game style, Kanly Melee.

- Kanly Melee: the Kanly timings (five minute round, then fast-rising water) with close-range weapons only. Bazooka,
  Homing Missile, Shotgun, Sniper Rifle, Poison Arrow, Sentry Gun, No More Nails, Bubble Trouble, Starburst, Super
  Sheep, Weapon Factory weapon, both airstrikes, Concrete Donkey, Fatkins, Alien Abduction, Scouser, Flood and Crate
  Shower are removed from the loadout and from crates. Fire Punch and Prod get 5 uses each (2 in Kanly).
- Fix to the docs: Kanly's 15 mystery crate entries get 10 ammo and no delay, they are not left as in Standard.
- Netplay: the host and every peer need Kanly 1.2.0, because the game style list positions shift with the new style.

## 1.1.0

Adds control fixes and control hints. Needs Melange 0.8.0 or later.

- Camera and aim: choose the 3rd-person camera Y direction (game, standard or inverted; standard is the new default),
  the first-person aim Y direction, whether the camera invert also applies in Blimp view, and a camera and an aim
  mouse sensitivity.
- Control hints: short, faded key and mouse prompts at the bottom centre on your own turn (off, learning or always).
  In learning mode a prompt disappears after you have used it three times.
- Controls legend: press F1 (or Ctrl+H, a setting) during a match for the full control list.
- A one-time note on the first match about the camera Y default.

## 1.0.2

Runs on Melange 0.7 and every later version: the plugin no longer names an upper Melange version, so a new
Melange release no longer moves it to `Mods\.incompatible`. No other change.

## 1.0.1

## 1.0.0

First release. Adds the Kanly game style and four Weapon Factory presets (Kanly Red, Blue, Green and Yellow) to the
vanilla menus, and replaces the sudden-death music with four Kanly tracks. Needs Melange 0.5.0 or later.
