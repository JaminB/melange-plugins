## 1.1.0

Adds control fixes and control hints. Needs Melange 0.8.0 or later.

- Camera and aim: choose the 3rd-person camera Y direction (game, standard or inverted; standard is the new default),
  the first-person aim Y direction, whether the camera invert also applies in Blimp view, and a camera and an aim
  mouse sensitivity.
- Control hints: short, faded key and mouse prompts at the bottom centre on your own turn (off, learning or always).
  In learning mode a prompt disappears after you have used it three times.
- Controls legend: press F1 (or Ctrl+H, a setting) during a match for the full control list.
- A one-time note on the first match about the camera Y default.

## 1.0.2

Runs on Melange 0.7 and every later version: the plugin no longer names an upper Melange version, so a new
Melange release no longer moves it to `Mods\.incompatible`. No other change.

## 1.0.1

Runs on Melange 0.6. No other change.

## 1.0.0

First release. Adds the Kanly game style and four Weapon Factory presets (Kanly Red, Blue, Green and Yellow) to the
vanilla menus, and replaces the sudden-death music with four Kanly tracks. Needs Melange 0.5.0 or later.
