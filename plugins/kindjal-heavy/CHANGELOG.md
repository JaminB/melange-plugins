## 1.1.0

- Covers the Buddy Bomb, the labradoodle that takes the Sheep's slot in Kindjal 1.3.0: Heavy scales his knockback like
  every other weapon's, 0.50 over 175 to 0.700 over 201.
- The dependency on Kindjal is now written `kindjal >=1.3.0`. 1.0.0 wrote it with a space (`kindjal >= 1.1.0`),
  which Melange could not read, so the add-on did not load. It now needs Kindjal 1.3.0 or later.

## 1.0.0

First release. Needs Melange 0.9.0 or later and Kindjal 1.1.0 or later.
