## 1.1.0

Foldspace's second map, Dustcrown. Needs Melange 0.15.0 or later (it was 0.14.0).

- Dustcrown: a water-starved desert of dunes and sandstone mesas around one vast palace. Sheer walls rise from a
  stepped base, past inner courtyards, to corner towers and a tall keep. There is no ramp: Jetpack, Ninja Rope,
  Teleport and Girder are the way in.
- Teams start in four sand troughs and the palace, dealt per match by the game from the match seed (two teams in
  opposite dunes, three as two in the sand and one on the palace, four as three in the sand and one on the palace).
- Built for Sandtide: the sea reads as sand, and the sand line leaves the palace above its lowest step and the two
  refuge mesas safe. Without Sandtide the map plays with the game style's own sudden death.
- Dustcrown always plays by day, with a light sand haze, and has its own picture in the map list.
- The map's rocks are one more mesh bank (Foldspace now uses 2 of the 44 mod mesh sections). They and the other detail
  are decoration only and never change a shot.
- Caldera is unchanged.

## 1.0.0

Foldspace's first release: big hand-built multiplayer maps made with Melange's Erg editor. Needs Melange 0.14.0 or later.

- The first map, Caldera: a volcanic island with a crater at its summit. A mine factory in the crater throws volleys of
  mines out across the island, onto the slopes, the beaches and sometimes into a castle, keeping off the trails.
- Four team castles on the shore (Strand, Ashgate, Harbour and Seawall). Every team starts in its own castle, and a
  switchback trail of pale stone climbs from each castle to the crater rim.
- The sea stays at the beaches until sudden death, then rises with the game style's own Raise Water.
- Basalt rocks (all in one mesh bank) and plants dress the slopes and beaches. They are decoration only and never change a shot.
- The maps ship as Erg source, and Melange builds them from your own game when it starts and before it launches the
  game, after every install or update. The plugin carries no game files.
- Content mod: every player in an online match needs the same version.
