## 1.0.0

First release: a recipe for Melange's local content importer that brings the 174 importable maps of Renewation HD
0.2A2 into your own game folder. Downloads the official zip from mod.worms.pro (or uses a copy you already have),
verifies its SHA-256, and generates map packs on your PC. Caravan itself contains no maps.
